Frequently Asked Questions ❓

How long does delivery take? +
Since every single item is 100% handcrafted patiently upon order placement, standard crafting + delivery time takes 14 to 15 business days across India.
Can I request custom colors or designs? +
Yes, absolutely! Click on the "Customise 🎨" button on any product card or use the WhatsApp chat to discuss custom color schemes, sizes, or personalized designs.
Are these crochet items washable? +
Yes, gently hand-wash in cold water with mild detergent and lay flat to dry in shade to keep yarn textures soft and beautiful.
